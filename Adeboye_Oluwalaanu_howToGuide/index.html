Laanu's Guide to 3D Modeling

In this guide, I will be recreating a vase (figure 1) in the popular 3D modeling application known as Blender. Rather than a detailed step by step tutorial explaining every key I pressed, I wanted to focus on my thought process and general workflow and provide some exposure to the expansive world of 3D computer graphics I discovered about two years ago.
